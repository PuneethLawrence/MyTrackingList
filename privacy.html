Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Collection & Storage

MyTrackingList uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore to manage account credentials and store user-saved watchlist items (movies, games, and reads). Personal user data is never sold or shared with external third parties.

2. Local Storage & Session Data

We utilize browser local storage and session cookies strictly for maintaining active authentication states and preserving your UI preferences across browsing sessions.

3. Third-Party Services

Media cover graphics and informational metadata are sourced from public APIs or third-party placeholder services. Form submissions (such as contact requests) are processed via Formspree.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your data or account privacy, feel free to reach out via our contact form on the main dashboard.
